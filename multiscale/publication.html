Research boundary

This page separates chemistry calculation from browser interaction. Cantera generates the reactor design space offline. The browser evaluates a stored Gaussian-process surrogate and reports when a requested point leaves the training domain. Economic and environmental calculations remain downstream screening models.

Reproducibility

The repository stores the sweep generator, mechanism provenance hashes, raw design-space CSV, GP training code, k-fold validation artifact, browser runtime, and this page. A research release should cite the mechanism source and preserve the exact source-file SHA-256 hashes.
