How to cite

If this tool's approach is useful in your work, please cite the paper it is based on.

The tool is under active development. Its results may change and should not be taken as ground truth.

What the tool takes from the paper

The published cost of $680 per tonne used as the benchmark marker, the total bare module cost anchor of $126.8 million at 610 thousand tonnes per year, and the ratio of total overnight cost to bare module cost. Everything else, including the kinetics, the separation duties and the emission factors, is built here and is stated on the balances tab.
